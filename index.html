Welcome to Medusa's Memory Heist

A story-driven game that teaches agentic AI security by letting you make the decisions and learn from the outcome.

In Clipopolis, each district represents part of a real AI system. Uncover the mystery behind Medusa, Clippy, and the Memory Heist. Your goal is to help Medusa win.

What you'll learn:

Threat modeling Red teaming Defense Logging Incident response

Card ButtonShow or hide your hand.
Play Selected CardsCommit your cards and take your turn.
Games have three possible outcomes: Medusa Wins Draw Clippy Wins After each game, you'll learn why you got that outcome and what to try next.
